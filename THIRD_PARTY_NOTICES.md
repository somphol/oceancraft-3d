# Third-Party Notices

OceanCraft 3D is a static browser application. It does not ship third-party
images, textures, 3D models or audio files. The following runtime libraries and
web fonts are referenced from public CDNs.

## Three.js

- **Package:** three
- **Version:** r128 (0.128.0)
- **License:** MIT
- **URL:** <https://threejs.org/>
- **Used for:** WebGL renderer, scene graph, geometries, materials, sprites,
  points, cameras, lights and fog.
- **CDN:** <https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js>

## OrbitControls

- **Package:** three/examples/js/controls/OrbitControls.js
- **Version:** r128 examples
- **License:** MIT
- **URL:** <https://threejs.org/docs/#examples/en/controls/OrbitControls>
- **Used for:** damped camera orbit, zoom and polar-angle constraints.
- **CDN:** <https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.128.0/examples/js/controls/OrbitControls.js>
