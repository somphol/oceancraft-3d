Crew briefing

How to fish the deep

01

Cast

Hold Cast or Space to build power. Release to throw the bobber. More power reaches deeper, rarer water.

02

Wait

Watch the bobber and sonar. A splash and a chime mean a fish has taken the lure. The bite window is short.

03

Fight

Hold Reel or Space to pull. Keep the needle in the green safe zone. Ease off when a surge hits.

04

Log it

Land 18 species across three rarities. Spend coins on gear, chase legendary catches, and save your log as JSON.
